# D2241 — Remove a smooth cone meridian from the blind-drill Top drawing

P2, rows [11](../../roadmap-rows/11.md) and [30](../../roadmap-rows/30.md). Base `8e259b592ddfd7be643aa8b58c7c295dcff506c8`. Fixes the named [#775](https://github.com/barocss/cad/issues/775) fixture.

- **FAIL on the base, native and desktop.** The unchanged native 32 × 24 × 20 mm plate has one Ø8 hole with cylindrical depth 9 mm and a 118° drill point. The native rim is complete. The Top view incorrectly draws `drill.point#1|drill.point#2#1` from `[6,9]` to `[10,9]` mm. There are two tessellation stations. The second is the singular apex, where the two incident meshes supply normals with opposite Y components of magnitude 0.35816. Both coedges lack pcurves. Actual Studio and its exported SVG show the radial line with hidden lines and centre lines off. A separate XML reader counts five Top lines and two 16 mm paper-radius half arcs. CPU rendering gives black at all three interior seam probes.
- **Decision.** For a two-station straight edge, recognize one complete native meridian only when both boundary rows bind the same edge, both surfaces use a collapsed cone rail, and their apex, axis and regular oriented normals agree. Use the regular interior derivative on that boundary. Keep actual tangent-view silhouettes. Fall back to the existing classifier if the binding or derivative is unavailable. Existing visibility, hidden-line settings, tangent-ink settings and tolerances remain in place.
