# D1985 — Recover one retired edge of a Constant Fillet before a downstream Through Hole

P1 rows 15 and 16, [#20](https://github.com/barocss/cad/issues/20). Extends D1984 so signed Constant Fillets with two or three distinct unmerged straight edges hold a retired slot for explicit user recovery even when downstream features follow. When the best-alike edge has a different identity, the ordinary Open verifier reports a pending choice instead of silently substituting the name. The existing confirmation path checks the full downstream chain before accepting. The unchanged reference keeps its authored name and signature, and the downstream feature stays intact. No change to variable, fractional, curved or merged references, the agent edit policy, History group replacement, or the file schema.

- **Previous Chrome and Model (`a17c3aaaa`):** when an independent top Through Hole (`n3`, R2 at `(20, 0)` mm on `n1.top`) followed the two-edge Constant Fillet (`n2`), `verifyReferences` marked `n1.side[e1]|n1.side[e4] → n1.side[e4]|n1.side[e5]` as an automatic substitution (`changes: 1`, `problems: 0`). Desktop Studio refused to open the file (`Your part is unchanged`) rather than holding `n2` for recovery; the previously open model remained unchanged.
- **Same-owner control:** narrowing the original 80 × 40 mm rectangle to 80 × 38 mm without replacing `e1` keeps both Fillet references and the downstream Through Hole intact with zero reference problems. Independent volume is `30,400 − 40π − 20(4 − π) = 30,320 − 20π` mm³.
- **Current Chrome:** retiring `e1 → e5` while keeping the 80 × 38 × 10 mm geometry opens cleanly with `n2` held (`edges: [n1.side[e1]|n1.side[e4], n1.side[e2]|n1.side[e3]]`, `suppressed: true`, `rematched: undefined`, original `sigs` preserved) and downstream Through Hole `n3` active on `b3`. The held solid is strict-valid with genus 1, two inward R2 hole half-cylinders at `(20, 0)` mm, zero fillet cylinders, and volume `30,400 − 40π` mm³. Previewing Candidate 1 highlights `n1.side[e4]|n1.side[e5]` on `b3` without changing the document or Undo/Redo. Explicit confirmation replaces only the retired slot, keeps `n1.side[e2]|n1.side[e3]` and its saved signature intact, and preserves `n3` verbatim; the rebuilt solid is strict-valid with genus 1, two outward R2 fillet cylinders at `(−18, −16)` and `(58, 18)` mm, two inward R2 hole half-cylinders at `(20, 0)` mm, and volume `30,320 − 20π` mm³. Undo restores the held Fillet with the active Hole; Redo, native Save and fresh Open preserve the confirmed result. Five captured desktop states, zero errors, and 1,233 unchanged frozen source paths.
- **Refusal controls:** (1) when a downstream Through Hole at `(−19.7, −17.7)` mm (R0.2) sits inside the unrounded corner while `n2` is held but misses the body once the R2 fillet is restored, explicit confirmation fails full-chain validation (`Reference not applied` / `the hole at (-19.7, -17.7) along z misses the target`) and leaves the held Fillet, original slots/signatures, Hole and Undo/Redo unchanged; (2) when a downstream Chamfer depends on `n2.fillet0|n1.top`, Studio Open refuses without substitution (`Your part is unchanged`), leaving the current model intact.
- **Focused checks:** 71 Model tests (`references`, `half-ring-fillet-reference`, `held-finish-recovery`, `band-surface`), 15 Studio `held-finish-replacement` tests, Model and Studio `type-check`, scoped ESLint, driver syntax, and `git diff --check`. Regression Chrome replays for D1984 (`partial-held-constant-fillet-browser.mjs`), D1870 (`three-held-chamfer-browser.mjs --fillet`), and D1902 (`held-chamfer-through-hole-browser.mjs`) also pass.
