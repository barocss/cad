# D2251 — Integrate the two represented closed arches

P0 [row 16](../../roadmap-rows/16.md) and [row 30](../../roadmap-rows/30.md) · Follow-up to [#777](https://github.com/barocss/cad/issues/777) · Base `230301da0`.

- **FAIL, base.** The Studio STEP derivatives from D2250 preserve their full native curves and strict closed topology. They nevertheless report 4.075846953150458 and 21.77297442063037 mm³ for the elevated and inserted representations of the same 100 mm wide, 15 mm deep arch. The closed prism has profile height `10 u (1 - u)` at `x = 100 u`. Direct Cartesian integration gives 2,500 mm³ and centroid [50, 1, 7.5] mm. Saving and reopening preserve the erroneous base measurements.
- **Decision.** Keep the original surface and curve data. For the compressed monotone spans already admitted in D2250, map the existing Gauss stations and weights through the original chord coordinate on each individual span. Planar cap integrals evaluate the original boundary derivative. A whole ruled patch can use the same map when its two rows or columns have the same weights and exact translation. Its derivative and basis evaluation remain on the original patch. A pairwise positive formula evaluates the inverse Jacobian. A station that collapses or misses its represented chord coordinate by more than 1e-8 declines the change of variable for that integration.
- **PASS, analytic checks.** The same STEP bytes now give 2,499.999999999982 and 2,499.9999999999964 mm³. Both centroids agree to 2e-13 mm. The regression compares the ten volume and moment integrals and surface area with direct Cartesian and arc-length formulas. Both surface parameter orders, reversed weights, exact STEP readback, unresolved station refusal and the previous GP/non-GP quadratic cases pass. Node 22.19.0 focused kernel results: 40 pass, zero fail.
- **PASS, actual desktop.** Headed Chrome reopened both original STEP files. Studio displayed 2,500 mm³ for each, selected the same arch, and preserved mass, centroid and original native coefficients through Project Save, Close, reload and Open. Each run recorded 1,302 unchanged source pins and no console errors.
- **NOT_RUN.** Arbitrary trimmed or nonmonotone rational patches, high-degree and non-C0 derived representations, external CAD/PDF, and mobile remain outside this qualification.
